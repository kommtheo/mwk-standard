# Contributing to MWK Standard

Thank you for your interest in contributing a translation of the MWK Reference Summary.

This document explains how the process works, what we ask of contributors, and what contributors receive in return.

---

## What We Are Looking For

We are looking for **idealist translators**: people who combine native fluency in their target language with a technical background (engineering, computer science, communication research, or related fields) — and who share the conviction that AI systems can be built to respect human self-determination.

## How to Contribute

**Step 1 — Open an Issue**
Before you start translating, open a GitHub Issue using the **Translation Request** template. This lets us confirm that no one else is already working on the same language and connect you with any existing contributors.

**Step 2 — Translate the Reference Summary**
The English Reference Summary (PDF) is in `reference-summary/en/`. Translate the full document into your target language. Please do not summarize or abridge — a complete translation is required for inclusion.

Key terms to translate consistently:

| English | Explanation |
|---------|-------------|
| Dignified Communication | The core concept — translate for meaning, not literal equivalence |
| Sender responsibility | The principle that the initiating party retains accountability |
| Permission / Offer / Action | The three architecture levels |
| Self-determination | The user's right to decide without coercion |

**Step 3 — Submit a Pull Request**
Place your translation in the correct folder (`translations/[language-code]/`) and submit a pull request. Include your name and a one-sentence bio as you would like it to appear in the published translation.

**Step 4 — Review**
Translations are reviewed for technical accuracy and linguistic quality. We may ask for revisions. The review is collaborative, not evaluative — we want to help you succeed.

---

## Quality Standards

- **Native fluency** in the target language is required.
- **Technical comprehension**: you should understand what pseudocode is and how dialog systems work.
- **Term consistency**: use the glossary above consistently throughout the translation.
- **No machine translation as final output**: machine translation may be used as a draft tool, but the final submission must reflect human judgment and fluency.

---

## What Contributors Receive

- Your name and a brief bio, credited in the published translation (PDF and any print version)
- One printed copy of **Dignity by Design** (the German original)
- Recognition in the book's next edition preface
